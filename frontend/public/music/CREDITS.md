## Processing

Recordings were converted to stereo MP3 at 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps, with loudness
normalization to -20 LUFS and a -2 dBTP peak target. Source metadata was removed.
The full tracks are retained; no seamless loop edit is claimed. Playback repeats
the selected track. The player starts off with a default volume of 15%.
