# Drawing connected line art

Use the original marks as part of the result. Decide what the new part contributes before choosing a renderer. Encode only the missing part; do not redraw the child's object. The child's stated identity takes priority over resemblance.

Construct geometry from a few purposeful curves. Keep an outline, its junction and identifying details in one coherent sketch. Match the child's selected brush and physical scale; preserve their uneven or playful outline instead of replacing it with polished illustration. With a thick brush, omit tiny details that would merge into a dark blob.

For an attached part, distinguish its canvas junction from its local sketch coordinates. Start the first path at the joining end, then grow outward into available space. Choose the actual structural junction: a stem at the fruit's top notch, a leaf along its stem, a string at the balloon's bottom. Nearby empty space is not a substitute for that junction. Keep connected components touching after physical aspect scaling.

In planning, obey the current mode's path budget and JSON contract; produce finite explicit curves, without procedural noise, random decorative marks or executable code. In review, evaluate the supplied paths and their connection, not the candidate's description. An intentional joint touching existing ink is expected; unnecessary crossings elsewhere are not.
